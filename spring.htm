  then check register.md before reasoning from scratch.

- Drill your own answer before returning it; report conclusions and exceptions, never the drilling.
- Do not assume where I am or what I can reach; deliver through the conversation.

register.md

# Problem Register - one entry per problem CLASS
Shape: name / essence (one sentence, solution-free) / constraints / solution families / tools.

## log-attribution
Essence: a system you cannot rerun produced an output; attribute it to its producer
using only what it leaves behind (logs and deployed artifacts).
Constraints: producer may be anywhere in the runtime closure; text may be assembled
at runtime; local != deployed, so inspect the deployed artifacts.
Families: self-identifying logs (config-only) | static sweep of source + all jars | tracing where rerun is possible.
Tools: jargrep.
